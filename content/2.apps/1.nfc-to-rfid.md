## What it does

**NFC to RFID** copies the UID of an NFC card onto a UHF EPC Gen2 tag, so a long-range UHF scan returns the same identifier
the card reports over NFC. Typical use: assets or badges carry both an NFC card (secure, short-range identification) and a UHF label
(bulk inventory from a distance).

- **Encode** - tap a card, confirm, and its UID is written into the tag's EPC; tags can be password-locked against tampering.
- **Scan** - bulk inventory of encoded tags with live card-tag pairing validation.
- **Validate** - tap a card and get an instant **PAIRED** / **TAG MISSING** verdict.
- **NXP originality check** - the card's silicon is verified against NXP public keys on every tap.

## Installation

On a Sunmi device install the app from the **Sunmi App Store** - open this link on the device:

```text
market://woyou.market/appDetail?packageName=dev.duma.android.nfctorfid
```

Alternatively download the APK from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/kduma-autoid/android-sunmi-nfc-to-uhf-rfid/releases/latest){:target="_blank"}
or build it yourself (JDK 17+): `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug`.

Requires Android 7.0+ with NFC and a supported UHF reader: the built-in module of a Sunmi L2s RFID / L3, the UHF trigger handle of a
Sunmi L2k / L2s / L2H / L2s Pro, or a Chainway reader (R2 / R3) over USB or Bluetooth.

## How it works

1. **Tap the NFC card** - the app reads its UID and checks the NXP originality signature.
2. **Check for one tag** - exactly one UHF tag must be in range, otherwise the workflow stops with an error.
3. **Confirm** - the current and the new EPC are shown, with an overwrite warning if the tag belongs to another card.
4. **Write, lock, verify** - the UID is written into the 96-bit EPC (`4E` + UID length + UID, non-GS1 namespace),
   the tag is locked with an access and a kill password and read back to verify.

The same EPC is later used by **Scan** and **Validate** to pair cards with tags. Feedback (tones, vibration, full-screen green/red flash)
is designed for warehouse use. The English and Polish interface is selected automatically.
